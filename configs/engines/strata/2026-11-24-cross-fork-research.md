# Cross-fork research — Qwen3.8 / RTX 3090 (2026-11-24)

Goal: ≥150 decode tok/s and +50% fresh prefill (~4230 tok/s at 32K); final validation at 131072 and near 262144. Same UD-Q4_K_XL weights, window 262144, sequential inference, PL ≤300 W, accessible sensors <70°C. No silent quality tradeoff.

Staging tests change outputs; limited shapes; not approved as drop-in replacement. Review integer MMVQ/MMQ dispatch and staging only with same weights and parity.
- bodhi37: https://github.com/bodhi37/strata/tree/95d7feecc8d178dc8aa0763310372960ca833300 — tiled prefill, fused CPU expert pool, early issuing and graph warmup. - Speedy 0.5.5: https://github.com/DevDock-AI/Speedy/tree/1f172963a0a483672381e74254b283df22c25e81 — reviewed actual configs/cards/rtx3080-20gb/1x/strata-ud-q4_k_xl.json and docs/SPEEDY_DEFAULTS.md/TUNING-HISTORY.md. EXCHANGE_ROTATE present but eligibility requires a fully pinned complement; inspect READY status.
- Upstream regression reports: https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata/issues/6887 (Ampere small-prompt CPU-share regression, candidate STRATA_PREFILL_CPU_SHARE_NT=0; not proven 32K+); https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata/issues/6799 (reported decode regression on some Ampere cards, not universal).
